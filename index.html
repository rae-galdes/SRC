Diversity, equity and inclusion

Inequity is rarely about one person or one decision. It's built into systems: how people are recruited, paid, promoted, developed and heard. I help organisations find where those systems create barriers, then change the system, not just the conversation.

DEI advisory

Strategy and practical advice grounded in your data and your people's experience, aimed at the parts of the system that shape outcomes.

  • Inclusion reviews of policy, process and systems
  • Gender equity analysis and action planning
  • DEI strategy and governance, including committees and employee networks
  • Inclusive recruitment and onboarding

DEI education

Learning that builds understanding and confidence, accessible from the start and connected to how work really happens.

  • Workshops for staff and leaders
  • Gender equity, gender diversity and trans inclusion
  • Neuroinclusion at work
  • Allyship and everyday inclusion

Leadership development

Leaders shape the system everyone else works in. Building their capacity to lead change, lead inclusively and make sound decisions.

  • Inclusive leadership programs
  • Leading through change
  • Psychological safety in teams

Why the system matters

Training on its own can't shift outcomes that recruitment, pay, progression and everyday practice keep producing. Looking at the whole system shows where a change will actually last.

Want to look at inclusion across your organisation as a whole? I'd welcome a conversation.

Ethical AI and digital capability

AI and digital tools land in a system that's already running: roles, workflows, policies, platforms and culture. I help organisations understand that system first, then build the understanding, people strategy, guardrails and learning that let new tools work safely and well.

Ethical AI education, adoption and workforce capability

Responsible AI understanding for leaders and teams, connected to the governance, roles and change support around it.

  • AI literacy programs by role
  • Responsible use guidelines and guardrails
  • AI governance and risk awareness
  • Adoption and change planning

Digital learning and enablement strategy

A clear plan for how people learn new tools and ways of working, and the learning and client platforms that hold it together.

  • Digital learning strategy and roadmaps
  • Enablement for system rollouts
  • LMS and CRM selection, implementation and integration
  • Data, reporting and migration

Instructional and learning experience design

Learning built around real tasks and real constraints, accessible by default, and designed to fit the system it lives in.

  • eLearning and blended programs
  • Job aids and performance support
  • Accessibility reviews of learning

Why the system matters

A new tool changes how work flows, who decides what, and where risk sits. Understanding those connections first gives adoption a real chance to last.

Planning for AI or a new platform? I'd welcome a conversation.

Talent and workforce strategy

Capability isn't just what individuals know. It's shaped by how you recruit, develop, structure, reward and keep people. I help organisations see how those parts connect, find where the gaps and risks sit, and build a plan to close them.

Talent and workforce strategy

How you attract, grow and keep people, seen as one connected system rather than separate processes.

  • Workforce planning and skills mapping
  • Attraction, development and retention strategy
  • Succession and career pathways

Capability frameworks and organisational development

A shared picture of what good looks like, built to connect recruitment, development, performance and planning.

  • Capability and competency frameworks
  • Organisation design and change
  • Performance and development processes

Organisational risk and capability advisory

Finding where capability gaps create risk across the system, and building a realistic plan to close them.

  • Capability gap and risk assessment
  • Compliance and mandatory learning review
  • Critical role and key person risk

Why the system matters

A skills gap in one team is often a signal from somewhere else: how roles are designed, how people are developed, or how knowledge is shared. Looking across the system shows where to act.

Thinking about capability across your organisation? I'd welcome a conversation.
