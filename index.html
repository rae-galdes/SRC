03 / ABOUT

The human side of technical solutions

Technology only works when it works for people. That's where I start.

I'm Rae, Principal Consultant at Sarah Rae Consulting, which I founded in 2020. Over more than 12 years I've worked across community services, registered training organisations and not-for-profits, and that grounding shapes every engagement.

My background in social welfare and organisational learning means I begin with the people in the system. Who has to use this? What do they need to do their best work? What gets in the way? Then I design the frameworks, learning and platforms to fit.

I bring people strategy and technical delivery together, so you don't have to translate between someone who understands your culture and someone who understands your systems.
